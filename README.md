# Nathan Lane

Economist at the London School of Economics, working at the intersection of economic
development, political economy, and applied machine learning. Co-founder of the
[Industrial Policy Group](https://industrialpolicygroup.com).

[Website](https://nathanlane.info) · [CV](https://nathanlane.info/cv.pdf) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-0884-8418)

## Selected work

- [Manufacturing Revolutions](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/1/403/7926978) — industrial policy and industrialization in South Korea's heavy and chemical industry drive. *QJE*, 2025.
- [Measuring Industrial Policy](https://www.nber.org/papers/w33902) — a text-based approach to measuring industrial policy at scale.
- [Industrial Policy and the Global Semiconductor Sector](https://www.nber.org/papers/w32892) — quantifying subsidies and market structure in semiconductors.
- [The New Economics of Industrial Policy](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-economics-081023-024638) — with Réka Juhász and Dani Rodrik. *Annual Review of Economics*, 2023.
