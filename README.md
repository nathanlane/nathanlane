# Nathan Lane

I'm an economist and professor at the [London School of Economics](https://www.lse.ac.uk/people/nathan-lane). I work at the intersection of economic development, political economy, and applied machine learning.

## Selected work

- [Manufacturing Revolutions](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjaf025) — industrial policy and industrialization in South Korea's heavy and chemical industry drive. *QJE*, 2025.
- [Measuring Industrial Policy](https://www.nber.org/papers/w33895) — a text-based approach to measuring industrial policy at scale.
- [Industrial Policy in the Global Semiconductor Sector](https://www.nber.org/papers/w32651) — quantifying subsidies and market structure in semiconductors.
- [The New Economics of Industrial Policy](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-economics-081023-024638) — with Réka Juhász and Dani Rodrik. *Annual Review of Economics*, 2024.
