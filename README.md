# Nathan Lane

I'm an economist and professor at the [London School of Economics](https://www.lse.ac.uk/people/nathan-lane). I work at the intersection of economic development, political economy, and applied machine learning. Command line enthusiast; open software and open weight fan.

I am faculty at the LSE [Department of International Development](https://www.lse.ac.uk/international-development). I co-direct the MSc Economic Policy for International Development. I am affiliated with the [Centre for Economic Performance (CEP)](https://cep.lse.ac.uk/) and the [Programme on Innovation and Diffusion (POID)](https://poid.lse.ac.uk/) at the LSE, and the [CESifo Research Network](https://www.cesifo.org) in Munich. I am a cofounder of [Sodalabs.io](https://sodalabs.io) in Melbourne.
